The Volkshaus

Volkshaus Zürich sits right at Stauffacher, one of the city's central transport hubs — reachable within a few minutes' walk via several tram and bus lines. The historic hall building provides the perfect setting for the whisky fair: generous space for over 35 exhibitors, a dedicated hall for the masterclasses, and stages for the concerts on both festival days.

The Organiser

Andreas Winkler was a soloist at Zürich Opera House for 15 years and has been a whisky expert for just as long. For 15 years he has organised the Whisky & Music Tastings — together with partners such as Acla da Fans, House of Single Malts, Scotch Malt Whisky Society, World of Whisky and Glenfahrn.

The biggest and most exclusive whisky festival in the heart of Zürich — 27–28 November 2026, Volkshaus Zürich.
