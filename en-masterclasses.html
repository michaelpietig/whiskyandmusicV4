Newly confirmed · Saturday 28.11.2026

Teeling — The Irish Whiskey Renaissance

CHF 49.-
5:00pm · Gelber Saal, Volkshaus

A carefully curated selection from Teeling — from young, experimental cask finishes to the rare 21-year-old Vintage Reserve.
Discover just how diverse and multi-faceted Irish whiskey can be.

In 2015, Teeling opened as Dublin's first new distillery in 125 years, reviving a city that was once the centre of the global whiskey trade. The company combines centuries-old Irish whiskey tradition with a spirit of innovation, extraordinary cask ageing and an unmistakably modern style.

Six selected bottlings will be tasted: Teeling Small Batch Rum Cask, Teeling Single Grain, Teeling Crystal Malt, Teeling 21yo Vintage Reserve Silver Bottling, Teeling 2017 8yo Cask #77552 Chestnut Cask, and Teeling Blackpitts 2018 5yo Marsala Cask.

The masterclass is led by Beibhinn O'Reilly, European Brand Ambassador for Teeling Whiskey. In March 2026, she was named "World's Best Irish Whiskey Brand Ambassador" at the Icons of Whisky/World Whiskies Awards.

Speaker Beibhinn O'Reilly, European Brand Ambassador Teeling WhiskeyLanguage EnglishDuration 50 min
