Go deeper, not just deeper into the glass

Teeling — The Irish Whiskey Renaissance

CHF 49.-
5:00pm · Gelber Saal, Volkshaus

A carefully curated selection from Teeling — from young, experimental cask finishes to the rare 21-year-old Vintage Reserve. Discover just how diverse and multi-faceted Irish whiskey can be.

In 2015, Teeling opened as Dublin's first new distillery in 125 years, reviving a city that was once the centre of the global whiskey trade. The company combines centuries-old Irish whiskey tradition with a spirit of innovation, extraordinary cask ageing and an unmistakably modern style.

Six selected bottlings will be tasted: Teeling Small Batch Rum Cask, Teeling Single Grain, Teeling Crystal Malt, Teeling 21yo Vintage Reserve Silver Bottling, Teeling 2017 8yo Cask #77552 Chestnut Cask, and Teeling Blackpitts 2018 5yo Marsala Cask.

The masterclass is led by Beibhinn O'Reilly, European Brand Ambassador for Teeling Whiskey. In March 2026, she was named "World's Best Irish Whiskey Brand Ambassador" at the Icons of Whisky/World Whiskies Awards.

The Duel

CHF 50.-
5:30pm · Gelber Saal, Volkshaus

Two whisky personalities go head to head in Zürich — with bottles, stories and pure passion:

Julie Hamilton — Sales & Brand Manager at Decadent Drinks (Scotland). She brings the celebrated independent bottlings of Angus MacRaild to Zürich for the first time: Whiskyland, Equinox & Solstice, Notable Age Statement, Decadent Drams and more. Julie is also head of the Glasgow Whisky Club and a judge at the international CMB Spirit Selection.

Joe Clark — Whisky Director at Spirit of Yorkshire (England). He presents the award-winning Filey Bay Single Malts — made "field to glass" in Yorkshire. 100% home-grown barley, pure spring water, sustainable production and a clear signature that unites lightness and depth.

Battle mode — a head-to-head comparison in the glass. The audience decides who takes the win home! 3 rounds — 2 whiskies per round (one each from Julie and Joe). Poured blind: two glasses per round, nobody knows which is which. Taste & compare: guests nose, taste and note their impressions. Reveal & storytelling: Julie and Joe reveal their whiskies and share the background. Audience vote: who wins the round — Scotland or England?

The running order: Round 1: The Gentle Touch — fresh entry-level drams. Round 2: The Character Clash — sherry-cask power vs. Decadent complexity. Round 3: The Heavyweights — single-cask Filey Bay against a hand-picked Decadent Drinks highlight. At the end, the audience crowns the Whisky Champion of Zürich!

Why join in? 6 exclusive whiskies, head to head. Two charismatic experts with insider knowledge and great stories. You are the jury — interactive, exciting and great fun. An evening full of enjoyment, knowledge and competitive spirit — but only one can become champion. Ready for the battle?

Speakers Julie Hamilton & Joe ClarkLanguage EnglishDuration 60 min

Let yourself be captivated by the extraordinary diversity and masterful craftsmanship of Japanese whiskies from the house of Suntory.

Our brand ambassador Bruno Schneeberger guides you on a sensory journey — from how it all begins at the distilleries to the distinctive character in the glass.

Ardnahoe — It's Islay Time!

CHF 45.-
Friday · Gelber Saal, Volkshaus

Discover Ardnahoe, the newest and one of the most fascinating distilleries on the legendary island of Islay off Scotland's west coast. Here, where tradition meets innovation, the Laing family brings three generations of expertise as blenders and independent bottlers into whisky production — a journey that began in 2018. Jim McEwan, a well-known figure in the whisky world, was also involved in planning this special distillery.

In our exclusive seminar, you'll have the chance to meet Andrew Laing, co-founder of Ardnahoe and director of Hunter Laing, in person. Andrew will explain the philosophy of Ardnahoe in accessible Scottish English and share fascinating insights into the art of whisky making. He explains how the longest lyne arms in Scotland and the worm-tub condenser shape the whisky's character, and what matters in selecting the casks.

Also enjoy a tasting of current and past peated single malts under Andrew's guidance — an excellent opportunity to learn everything about Ardnahoe first-hand. Join us when it's time to say: "It's Islay Time!"

Speaker Andrew Laing, Co-Founder ArdnahoeLanguage EnglishDuration 60 min

Inside Amrut — with Pramod Kashyap

CHF 35.-
6:30pm · Gelber Saal, Volkshaus

Immerse yourself in the fascinating world of Indian single malts at our exclusive Amrut masterclass at the Whisky & Music Festival. This special session is led by Amrut brand ambassador Pramod Kashyap, travelling especially from India. DramCatcher — 223 Years in 6 Whiskies

CHF 95.-
6:30pm · Gelber Saal, Volkshaus

Six whiskies with a combined age of 223 years, every one a single cask bottled at cask strength! That really says it all. Thomas and Stephan, the guys behind DramCatcher, have always set out to give their whisky friends an unforgettable experience at every visit to their stand and every one of their tastings. So it was a matter of honour for both of them to put together a line-up for this tasting like no other. They've assembled their oldest and most precious whiskies from the DramCatcher range. Every single one over 30 years old, five of them single malts. Picking one highlight is simply impossible. How would you choose from this line-up? North British Single Grain 32yo, Glenrothes Single Malt 31yo, Tormore Single Malt 33yo, Littlemill "Lost Distillery" Single Malt 34yo, Isle of Jura Single Malt 43yo, Glenburgie Single Malt 50yo…

All whiskies are either DramCatcher's own bottlings or releases from Brave New Spirits, for which DramCatcher is the exclusive importer for Switzerland. "BNS" will also be on site, and with a bit of luck there may be a surprise or two.

Thomas and Stephan are looking forward to welcoming you to this first-class tasting!

Masterclass InchDairnie Distillery

CHF 49.-
8:00pm · Gelber Saal, Volkshaus

Join us for an exclusive journey into the world of flavour innovation with InchDairnie Distillery. This unique masterclass invites whisky enthusiasts to discover and taste a selection of remarkable expressions crafted at one of Scotland’s most forward-thinking distilleries.

Founded by industry visionary Ian Palmer, InchDairnie Distillery is built on over 40 years of expertise and a passion for pushing boundaries. As Operations Manager at Invergordon Distillery and Group Distilling Manager for all the distilleries in the Whyte & Mackay portfolio — including The Dalmore, Jura, Bruichladdich, Fettercairn and Tamnavulin — Ian’s influence has shaped Scotch whisky far beyond InchDairnie. His contributions to the industry have been recognised with the prestigious title of Master of the Quaich in 2022.

At InchDairnie, tradition meets innovation. Equipped with state-of-the-art stills and bespoke engineering, the distillery explores new dimensions of flavour that cannot be achieved with conventional methods. Every whisky is meticulously researched, developed, and distilled with the aim of unlocking tastes rarely found in Scotch.

During this masterclass, you will taste a curated range of InchDairnie whiskies, each showcasing the distillery’s signature innovation, learn about the unique production techniques that make InchDairnie stand apart, and hear the story behind the distillery’s creation and vision for the future of Scotch.

Whether you’re a seasoned whisky connoisseur or just beginning your journey, this is a rare opportunity to experience the spirit of innovation in every dram. Raise a glass with us — and taste the future of Scotch whisky.

Speaker Ian Palmer, Founder InchDairnie DistilleryLanguage EnglishDuration 60 min
