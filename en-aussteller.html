Exhibitors

35+ bottlers, importers & distilleries

From international rarities to Swiss whisky specialities – extraordinary bottlings and exciting discoveries.

ACLA da Fans

678 different bottlings, duty-free shopping 8 kilometres from Samnaun, and legendary whisky tastings by Lake Zürich for many years – Acla da Fans has long become a fixture in the single malt world. As an independent bottler, Acla da Fans has a sure touch, regularly earning top ratings on the relevant tasting sites with its own bottlings and joint bottlings.

Brennerei zum Tröpfli

Brennerei zum Tröpfli in Obfelden combines history with modern whisky craftsmanship. For seven years, the distillery has produced its single malt entirely in-house — from milling the malted barley through brewing and distilling to maturation in its own cellar. Brewing and distilling are carbon-neutral.

C. Dully

Christian Dully and his colleagues specialise in cask-strength bottlings. They travel all year round to find and bottle great spirits at fair prices. While their original focus was on single malt Scotch whisky, they now also seek out other spirits of exceptional quality.

Cask Brothers

Cask Brothers GmbH from Lenzburg stands for carefully selected whiskies and the highest quality. The company specialises in importing and distributing special bottlings for anyone who wants to enjoy whisky mindfully. As the official Swiss importer of the Annandale distillery, Cask Brothers also carries exclusive product lines such as World of Orchids, as well as selected bottlings from Ferguson Whisky. The focus is on character, provenance and authenticity. The range includes hand-picked single malts, limited editions and special single-cask bottlings with a distinctive profile. The offering is rounded off with whisky tastings that combine enjoyment and expertise.

Catawiki

Catawiki, the curated online auction platform for extraordinary items, brings a selection of exquisite whisky rarities to the festival. On site, visitors can discover rare bottlings and taste very special drams that don't come along every day.

Charles Hofer SA

For almost 35 years, our brands have been our hallmark. The whisky portfolio of Charles Hofer SA brings together whiskies mainly from independent distilleries and bottlers. The following brands are presented:

Scotland: Arbikie, Arran, As We Get It, Berrys’ Own Selection, Cù Bòcan, GlenAllachie, Glengoyne, Glenturret, House of McCallum, Kilkerran, Lagg, Lochlea, Machrie Moor, MacNair’s, Smokehead, Six Isles, Tomatin
Europe: Brenne, Cotswolds, Penderyn, The Whistler
USA & Canada: Bib & Tucker, FEW, Widow Jane, Masterson’s, Redemption
Japan: Fujimi, Fuyu, Hinotori, Kurayoshi, Matsui, Tottori

Diwisa

Alongside well-known classics from Glenfarclas Distillery, this year we're also bringing interesting single-cask bottlings from the Family Cask range. Further Scotch whiskies from Glen Moray Distillery, as well as Irish malts from the new Dublin Liberties Distillery, will be available to taste at our stand. The portfolio is rounded off with limited bottlings from Japan's Mars Distillery, American specialities and Swiss whisky.

DramCatcher

DramCatcher is back again this year – bringing pure whisky enjoyment to the festival! Behind DramCatcher are Thomas Raz and Stephan Manfré, two whisky enthusiasts with over 15 years of experience. They seek out and find casks of exceptional quality, which they bottle directly – without additives, colouring or chill filtration. Whisky in its purest form! Since 2025, DramCatcher has also been the exclusive Swiss importer of the award-winning whiskies from Brave New Spirits in Glasgow, an independent bottler and owner of the new Witchburn Distillery in Campbeltown (production due to start autumn 2025). "DrC" will once again bring some exciting rarities along.

Formel Lau Thx

Own top bottlings and rarities.

Frozza Premium Spirits

A new, innovative company with over 25 years of whisky expertise. Our passion for fine spirits shows in every bottle — especially in our limited Single Cask series, which delights whisky lovers and collectors alike. Discover the depth and variety of our whiskies and exclusive import bottlings together with us.

Glen Fahrn

For over 30 years, we have been searching for special and rare spirits from around the world. With over 1,000 general import rights for niche products and special bottlings, plus a range of around 3,000 premium spirits, Glen Fahrn has (almost) everything to delight the heart of the connoisseur and enthusiast alike.

Hautes Glaces

Hautes Glaces is, above all, a return to the source – to the land. Founded over 15 years ago, Hautes Glaces is driven by the ambition to highlight the importance of grain in the craft of whisky-making – bringing its quality, provenance and character to the fore, and making them tangible in flavour and vitality. From our extensive range, we present the latest offerings from the long-established independent bottler Meadowside Blending of Glasgow, known for The Maltman and The Grainman. Also on show: the bottlings newly available in Switzerland from the Irish distillery Lough Gill, sold under the name Athrú.

House of Single Malts

From our extensive range, which leaves nothing to be desired, we present the newest treasures from the long-established independent bottler Meadowside Blending from Glasgow, known for The Maltman and The Grainman. Also featured: the newly available Swiss releases from the Irish distillery Lough Gill, marketed under the name Athrú.

Japanese Liquid Gold

Highly exclusive Japanese whisky rarities to buy and taste.

KesselHerz

A team of passionate connoisseurs, united by a love of uncompromising quality and authentic taste experiences. With great passion we create our own premium spirits — from characterful rum to fruity liqueurs and direct-trade coffee. Our credo is simple: we only create what we ourselves love to enjoy. Discover innovative creations, honest craftsmanship and special moments of pleasure.

Langatun

The legendary Swiss Langatun whisky is distributed worldwide: the high quality of Langatun's products and its passion for whisky have earned the distillery recognition from companies all over the world.

Lateltin

Since 1899, Lateltin has stood for passion, tradition and a spirit of innovation in the Swiss spirits market. At the Whisky & Music Festival Zürich, we present a select line-up of international premium whiskies and exciting new releases. Experience the fascination of our brands such as Bruichladdich and Tomintoul, plus many more premium whiskies from our portfolio. Our experts are on hand for tastings and personal conversations – with plenty of stories about the origin, production and character of our whiskies.

Los Hermanos

We import products from various regions of Europe directly from the producers. We personally select every product, because it should be high-quality, fair and sustainable. As general importer of Nc'nean, Eden.Mill, James Eadie, Ahascragh Distillery, Fyne Ales and Jump Ship Brewing, we bring character and quality to the glass. Joining us at the stand: brand ambassadors Suzanne Riviere from Nc'nean and Laura Tobin from Ahascragh Distillery.

MCR Spirits

Whisky is more than a drink – it's living history in every drop. At MCR Spirits, we are constantly on the lookout for special whiskies that tell exactly these stories. Our range unites character, provenance and craft – including a limited single-cask series that delights whisky lovers and collectors alike.

Naegelis Premium Products

We are a Swiss-Peruvian couple who, after an intensive search for exclusive Peruvian products, founded Naegelis Premium Products in 2023 — a company dedicated exclusively to importing Peruvian spirits into Switzerland. At the Whisky & Music Festival Zürich, we're excited to present the first Andean whiskey from Peru, made from purple corn. Even in pre-Columbian times, the Incas used purple corn to make fermented chicha de jora, a sacred drink for religious ceremonies and important celebrations. Our products are exclusive and handcrafted, and every bottling tells its own story — a story of origin, tradition and the cultural value behind every single bottle.

Nordic Drinks

Nordic Drinks presents exclusive Nordic whisky highlights at the Whisky & Music Festival: the Feddie Ocean Single Malt from Norway has recently become available in Switzerland. Alongside it, the latest bottlings from Aurora Spirits Distillery in Norway and Eimwerk Distillery in Iceland will be on show.

Ondjaba – The Namibian Whiskey

The name Ondjaba comes from the Oshiwambo language and means "elephant". For our editions we use various grains such as corn, barley, wheat, rye and mahangu (pearl millet), smoked over elephant dung. The whiskey then matures under the Namibian sun in a range of casks — including our own former wine and fortified-wine casks, bourbon casks and new oak casks. Our goal is a world-class whiskey with an unmistakably Namibian character — not a copy of Scotch or Irish whisky, but a distinct taste experience of its own. Maturing at over 1,200 metres altitude with strong temperature swings shapes its special character even further.

Paul Ullrich AG

Paul Ullrich AG is a dynamic, continuously growing third-generation family business that combines tradition with modernity. As an independent specialist retailer for wines and spirits in Switzerland, we offer our customers an exceptionally good and wide selection of wines and spirits from around the world. Our range includes around 2,000 whiskies, and our customers include restaurateurs, selected resellers and private clients.

Rémy Cointreau

The Rémy Cointreau Group, whose origins in the Charente region date back to 1724, was formed in 1990 from the merger of the holding companies of the Hériard Dubreuil and Cointreau families, which controlled E. Rémy Martin & Cie SA and Cointreau & Cie SA respectively. The Group is also the result of successive partnerships with other companies operating in the same wine and spirits business.

Säntis Malt

After the ban on distilling spirits from grain was lifted in 1999, Appenzell brewmaster Karl Locher, known for his creative beers, began producing Säntis Malt in historic oak beer casks using spring water from the Alpstein massif. It is precisely this malt extract from casks up to 130 years old that gives Säntis Malt whisky its unique note, passing on age-old beer memories.

Seven Seals

Numerous awards at the world's most renowned whisky competitions show that flavour isn't guaranteed by age alone, but through this special process. Every Seven Seals whisky is legally allowed to call itself whisky before it undergoes the short, effective Fast Forward Finishing process.

Speer-Braui

Speer-Braui from Kaltbrunn is a small Swiss distillery with roots in beer brewing. From this craft foundation came our Speer Single Malt Whisky – distilled in the Scottish tradition, matured in select oak casks, and always bottled in small batches. Every edition shows its own character, reflecting the vintage, cask and climate at the foot of the Speer, Europe's highest Nagelfluh mountain.

The Nose

In the heart of the Italian Alps, beside the Ortler mountain range, an extraordinary distillery was built. The PUNI Distillery, Italy's first whisky distillery, found its home in the Vinschgau valley, in the picturesque town of Glurns. The distillery continues to grow and expand its range of whiskies, today distilling 100% pure barley malt sourced mainly from Italy alongside peated malt from Scotland. For a varied range, PUNI whisky matures in a wide variety of casks, including ex-bourbon casks from the USA, ex-wine casks from Italy, ex-sherry casks from Spain, ex-peated casks and virgin oak. As a partner, Karl Hofer's The Nose GmbH handles distribution and sales for Switzerland.

Trompeterschlössle

The whisky hotel on Lake Constance: the Art Nouveau villa in Tägerwilen, built in 1904, was transformed into a stylish whisky hotel in 2021. Guests can look forward to the Pot Still Bar with over 400 fine whiskies, as well as enjoyable Whisky Talk & Dinner events. International experts lead engaging tastings, always with a fresh focus. A place for anyone who loves whisky and appreciates evenings with style and character.

Whisky Shop Neumarkt

Since 1 February 2019, Whisky Shop Neumarkt has been run by Reto Stöckli, following his four years working in the whisky industry on location in Scotland. His company Selection Trade GmbH continues to import selected Scotch whiskies and distribute them across Switzerland.

Whisky Time

Whisky Time is the only German-language specialist magazine for a large and steadily growing number of enthusiasts of "uisge beatha," the water of life. Their passion and dedication, as well as their insatiable curiosity for background information, current trends and new releases, form the foundation of our magazine, published since 1997. This makes us one of the oldest and leading whisky magazines in the world.

Whisky-Bibliothek

We stand for special experiences around single malt whisky – with expertise, passion and a curated selection of extraordinary bottlings. As the exclusive importer of whiskies from Spirit of Yorkshire Distillery (Filey Bay Single Malts) and the independent brand Decadent Drinks by Angus MacRaild, one of Scotland's most distinguished whisky experts, we bring authentic whiskies with character and provenance to Switzerland.

Whisky-Hood

Whisky-Hood presents the cask-strength single-cask bottlings of the Rieggers Selection. Also on show are further exclusive bottlings from its independent bottlers "The Whisky Cask," "The Pearls of Scotland" and "Brothers in Malt." Wine Attractions GmbH

Wine Attractions GmbH is your partner for fine wines, spirits and extraordinary foods. We offer a carefully curated selection of high-quality, innovative products from smaller, often family-run producers, with a focus on Italy, Austria, Germany, South Africa and Canada. We are particularly proud of our exclusive partnership with Stillhead Distillery, known for outstanding rye whisky, gin, vodka and liqueurs, as well as with Cosman & Webb, who mature a version of their traditional organic maple syrup in rye whisky casks (naturally from Stillhead). Both producers share our passion for the extraordinary.

World of Whisky

World of Whisky is the institution of the Swiss whisky scene and appears exclusively at the Whisky & Music Festival. This year, World of Whisky also presents the portfolio of Pernod Ricard Swiss, including the powerhouse brands Redbreast, Aberlour, Longmorn, Scapa, Midleton, Ballantine’s, Chivas Regal, Jameson and The Glenlivet. Also on show: the products of Dettling and Marmot – featuring The Macallan, Glenfiddich, The Balvenie, Benriach, The GlenRothes, Tullamore Dew, Jack Daniels and Woodford Reserve.
